Download image from https://www.fileformat.info/info/unicode/block/ using your hands or Font_downloader.
Now, you can modify image files if you want to adjust it.
Then resize your pictures to smaller resolutions (e.g. 25x25) by image resizer (or similar software).
After that open Padding_remover and select all pictures to remove unnecessary transparent paddings.

Convert your image to .t3x format, command line prefix is (you can tweak settings if needed) : tex3ds -o {output}.t3x -f a4 -z auto --atlas -p preview.png {input} {input1} {input2}...
on windows :
C:\devkitPro\tools\bin\tex3ds -o {output}.t3x -f a4 -z auto --atlas -p preview.png {input} {input1} {input2}...

Don't forget to update font_name.txt in romfs/gfx/msg, #define DEF_EXFONT_NUM_OF_FONT_NAME, #define DEF_EXFONT_NUM_OF_*_BYTES_FONT, util_exfont_font_characters,
samples_*_bytes in exfont.c/exfont.h and font load/unload button in setting_menu.c!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!
